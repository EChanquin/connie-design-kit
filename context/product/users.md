# Users — Connie

## Primary persona: Maya
- 34, seven months pregnant, buying her first stroller
- Works full-time, browses on her laptop in the evenings
- Active in a parents' Facebook group and on Instagram
- Trusts friends' word of mouth, but is data-curious
- Entry state: three weeks into research, eleven tabs open, no decision. Skeptical of new tools but wants a shortcut

## What she needs from this card
This card is the hero moment of the journey (Beat 3, "Investigate"). On an Instagram-famous stroller, she sees a marketing claim and wants to know if it's real. The card should leave her feeling sharp, not sold to.

## Design implications
- **Clarity over completeness.** One verdict, one sentence of why, then the evidence. She is scanning, not reading.
- **Source is everything.** She needs to see instantly which quote is CR's lab and which is a real parent. Never blend them.
- **Uncertainty is a feature.** "Unable to verify" is an honest answer, not an error state. It should look calm, not broken.
- **She stays in control.** The card informs, it never pushes a purchase. No buy buttons, no urgency.
